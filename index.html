THE NEXT GENERATION

Youth Theatre at ACT

Theatre gives young people far more than a place under the spotlight. It builds confidence, friendships, communication, responsibility, creativity and the courage to try something difficult in front of a room full of people.

FROM THE WINGS

A Monthly Note from ACT

“Theatre is built long before the audience takes their seats.”

Every production begins quietly. Someone chooses a story. Someone opens a script. Someone measures a piece of lumber, searches for a costume, learns a line or walks through our doors for the very first time.

This is where we'll share a new message each month from one of the people helping keep ACT moving forward.
