Young Performers

ACT Youth Theatre

Atascosa County Troupe is proud of our annual Summer Youth Productions, where we work with students ages 6–18, including seniors graduating that current year, to create youth-only productions built entirely around young performers.

Yes, they learn a play. Yes, they get a few glorious hours in the air conditioning during a South Texas summer. But it becomes much more than rehearsal.

Our students build friendships, confidence, and a theatre family while strengthening reading skills, teamwork, public speaking, responsibility, communication, and time management. Our older students also get opportunities to practice skills that carry into school, college, interviews, jobs, and everyday life, including leadership, collaboration, problem-solving, preparation, and working toward deadlines.

Feeding Our Youth

For the past two years, ACT has also provided dinner for our youth performers at each rehearsal. We want our students focused on learning, creating, and enjoying their time together, not worrying about whether they ate before rehearsal.

Want to help provide meals for our students? Visit our Support ACT section to learn how you can help.

Our Story

Community theatre since 1974.

Atascosa County Troupe has been part of the local arts community for generations. ACT brings people together through performance, storytelling, education, and the shared experience of live theatre.

Experienced performer or first-timer, backstage builder or enthusiastic audience member, there is room for you at ACT.
