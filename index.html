Ages 6–18 · Graduating seniors welcome

More Than a Summer Show

A Place to Belong.

ACT's Summer Youth Theatre brings young people together to rehearse, build, create and perform productions of their own.

Along the way they practice reading, public speaking, teamwork, responsibility, communication, problem solving, preparation and time management.

Older students have opportunities to develop leadership and collaboration skills that carry into school, college, interviews, jobs and life beyond the stage.

Dinner Is Part of Rehearsal

Sometimes taking care of young performers starts with making sure they have eaten.

For the past two years, ACT has provided dinner for our youth at rehearsals.

It has become an important part of how we care for the young people who spend their summer evenings with us.

Our Story

The Story Began in 1974.

Atascosa County Troupe has been part of the local community for more than half a century.

Across those decades, actors have stepped onto our stage, directors have imagined new worlds, volunteers have built them, audiences have filled the seats and generations of families have made memories here.

We're working to gather ACT's history into one place: past productions, photographs, programs, posters, milestones and stories from the people who were there.

Every old program, faded photograph and backstage story is another piece of ACT.

Have something from ACT's history that belongs here? We'd love to hear from you.

Who We Are

Theatre Lives Here.

Atascosa County Troupe is a nonprofit community theatre in Pleasanton, Texas.

Since 1974, ACT has brought performers, volunteers, audiences, families and young people together through live theatre and community events.

You don't need years of theatre experience to belong here. Sometimes all it takes is walking through the door.
