Fall Mainstage Production

Poe: Dreams of Madness

By Allyssa Hynes

Produced by special arrangement with Pioneer Drama Service Inc., Denver, Colorado.

Six performances. Two weekends. One descent into madness.

What happens when the master of the macabre becomes trapped inside his own imagination?

It is nearly midnight, and Edgar Allan Poe cannot sleep. Or perhaps he has been sleeping all along.

Haunted by dreams that follow him into waking life, Poe finds himself wandering through the darkest corners of his literary creations, guided by none other than the Raven himself.

The dead refuse to remain buried. A guilty heart beats beneath the floorboards. A pendulum swings ever closer. And the line between reality and nightmare grows dangerously thin.

In this award-winning theatrical adaptation, the haunting beauty and psychological terror of Poe's classic literature come alive through his most celebrated Gothic works, including The Tell-Tale Heart, The Fall of the House of Usher, The Pit and the Pendulum, The Masque of the Red Death, The Raven, and more.

Both familiar and unexpectedly original, this production weaves obsession, guilt, grief, madness, and mortality into an evening of suspense and unsettling theatre.

Will Poe ever escape the nightmares he created? And once you've entered his world, will you?

A History Worth Telling · Coming Soon

Every Stage Has a Story.

Since 1974, Atascosa County Troupe has been bringing stories to life. Now, we're working on telling one of our most important stories: our own.

And as you might imagine, gathering more than fifty years of theatre history is no small production!

We're digging through old programs, photographs, newspaper clippings, and memories to uncover the people and performances that made ACT what it is today.

We want to introduce you to our founding members, revisit our earliest productions, celebrate the people who helped shape this theatre, and explore how ACT has grown and changed through the decades.

Not just names and dates, but the stories behind them. The funny moments, meaningful milestones, familiar faces, and memories worth preserving.

The history of ACT isn't just about the shows we've performed. It's about the people who made them possible.

About ACT

A little theatre with a long history and an open door.

Stories Bring Us Together.

Atascosa County Troupe, known to friends and neighbors as ACT Theatre, is a nonprofit community theatre serving Pleasanton and the surrounding area.

Since 1974, ACT has offered a home for live storytelling, creativity, collaboration, and community connection.

We believe theatre belongs to everyone: performers, volunteers, families, first-time audience members, and longtime supporters alike.

Our productions and programs are made possible by the dedication of people who believe local theatre matters.
